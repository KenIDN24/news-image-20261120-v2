## Lead — Shenzhen
At the APEC gathering in Shenzhen, the American, Chinese and Russian
presidents met face to face for the first time as a trio and signed a
four-point peace declaration: reform of the United Nations, secured
refined-petroleum supply lines, limits on the reach of the World
Court and state immunity, and a shared pledge that a nuclear war must
never be fought. Ukraine was named the first priority.

## Second — The Hague
The International Court of Justice ruled 9-6 that Myanmar's army
committed genocide against the Rohingya in Rakhine State in 2017,
when roughly ten thousand people were killed. The court ordered
reparations and a safe return for the displaced. It is the first time
a state has been held responsible for genocide in the court's history.
